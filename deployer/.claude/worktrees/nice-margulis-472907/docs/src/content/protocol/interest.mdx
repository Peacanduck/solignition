# Interest math

<p className="lede">
  Interest is linear and time-prorated. There is no compounding. The admin fee is paid upfront and separate from interest.
</p>

<Callout kind="tldr">
  **tl;dr —** `interest = principal × interest_rate_bps / 10_000 × elapsed_seconds / duration_seconds`. Early repay is cheaper. Late repay isn't possible.
</Callout>

## The formula

When you call `repay_loan`, the program computes:

```
elapsed = now - loan.start_ts
interest = (principal × interest_rate_bps × elapsed) / (10_000 × duration)
total_owed = principal + interest
```

All in lamports, all `u64` arithmetic, all on-chain.

## Worked example

A borrower opens a 30-day loan at the protocol default rate:

- `principal` = 4 SOL = `4_000_000_000` lamports
- `interest_rate_bps` = 500 (5%)
- `duration` = `2_592_000` seconds (30 days)

If they repay after 5 days (`elapsed = 432_000`):

```
interest = (4_000_000_000 × 500 × 432_000) / (10_000 × 2_592_000)
         = 33_333_333 lamports
         ≈ 0.0333 SOL
```

If they repay at the very end of the term (`elapsed = duration`):

```
interest = (4_000_000_000 × 500 × 2_592_000) / (10_000 × 2_592_000)
         = 200_000_000 lamports
         = 0.2 SOL
```

So a 30-day loan at 5% costs at most 5% of the principal in interest. Held for 25% of the term, you pay 25% of that max — 0.05 SOL.

## Why linear

Compounding interest is a footgun for short-term loans on a per-block clock. Linear interest:

- Caps the upside at `principal × rate`, which is easy to reason about.
- Makes early repay strictly cheaper (no penalty term).
- Doesn't require frequent on-chain state updates.

## The admin fee is separate

The admin fee runs on a different mechanism:

- Paid in full at `request_loan`, debited from the borrower's wallet.
- Stored in `Loan.admin_fee_paid` for the record.
- Does **not** accrue. Does **not** refund on early repay.
- Distributed at `repay_loan` (or `recover_loan` on default) via `admin_fee_split_bps`.

Practically: the admin fee is the protocol's origination cost. Interest is the LP's yield.

## Where it runs

The math lives in the `repay_loan` handler in the Anchor program. Watch for:

- **`MathOverflow` (error 6018)** — possible if `principal × interest_rate_bps × elapsed` overflows `u64`. At realistic SOL amounts this is unreachable, but custom builds with huge principals could hit it.
- **`LoanExpired` (error 6020)** — interest is only payable inside `[start_ts, start_ts + duration]`. After that, repay is closed and the loan moves toward recovery.
