# Shares & yield

<p className="lede">
  Shares are a fixed-supply claim on a growing pool. Your share count doesn't change between deposits and withdrawals; the SOL per share goes up as borrowers repay.
</p>

<Callout kind="tldr">
  **tl;dr —** Each repay adds SOL to the vault. Your `share_amount` stays put. When you withdraw, shares redeem at the current ratio: `payout = your_shares × vault_balance / total_shares`.
</Callout>

## The model

If you've used Compound's cTokens or an ERC-4626 vault, it's the same idea:

- **Total shares** (`ProtocolConfig.total_shares`) only changes on `deposit` and `withdraw`.
- **Vault balance** (the SOL held by the `["vault"]` PDA) grows on every repay, every default recovery, and every admin-fee credit.
- **Share price** = `vault_balance / total_shares`. Climbs over time. Doesn't go down except on principal loss — and the protocol has no principal-loss mechanism (defaults are covered by the deployed program's reclaimed rent + the borrower's forfeited admin fee).

No rebase. No token mint. Your `DepositorRecord.share_amount` is a `u64` that doesn't move until you redeem.

## Where the yield comes from

Three sources, all feeding into the vault:

1. **Borrower interest at repay.** `interest = principal × interest_rate_bps / 10_000 × elapsed / duration`. The LP share is `interest × admin_fee_split_bps / 10_000`. Default split = 50%, so LPs get half.
2. **Borrower admin fee.** Paid upfront at `request_loan`, split the same way. LPs get their share whether the loan repays cleanly or defaults.
3. **Reclaimed program rent.** On default, the deployer closes the borrower's program and `return_reclaimed_sol` pushes that SOL back into the vault.

## A worked example

Suppose the vault has `100 SOL` and `1_000_000_000` total shares. You hold `100_000_000` shares (10%).

A borrower repays a loan with `0.2 SOL` of interest. At the default split, `0.1 SOL` lands in the vault.

- New vault balance: `100.1 SOL`.
- Total shares: unchanged (`1_000_000_000`).
- Your shares: unchanged (`100_000_000`).
- Your claim: `100_000_000 × 100.1 / 1_000_000_000 = 10.01 SOL`.

You earned `0.01 SOL` without doing anything.

## On default

When a loan expires unrepaid:

- The borrower's `admin_fee_paid` (already in the protocol) is distributed via the standard split — LPs get their share.
- The deployer closes the program. The rent (typically the bulk of the loan's principal) goes back into the vault via `return_reclaimed_sol`.

So a default isn't a loss event for LPs — it's just a slower path where the borrower's program gets dismantled instead of repaid. The LP yield on a defaulted loan equals their share of the admin fee, not the full interest.

## APY

The protocol does **not** publish a fixed APY. Yield is variable and depends on:

- **Utilization** — how much of the vault is out as active loans. Idle SOL doesn't earn anything.
- **Borrower rates** — the minimum is 500 bps, but borrowers can request higher rates. Most won't.
- **Loan mix** — 7-day loans turn over faster than 90-day loans, so total fees per year per SOL is higher.
- **Default rate** — see above; defaults still yield admin-fee share + reclaimed rent.

To estimate your APY in practice: track `share_amount × vault_balance / total_shares` over time. The slope is your realized yield.

## Tracking your position

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `DepositorRecord.share_amount` | Your share balance. |
| `DepositorRecord.deposited_amount` | Cumulative lamports you've deposited (history, not current value). |
| `ProtocolConfig.total_shares` | Denominator for the share-to-SOL ratio. |
| `ProtocolConfig.total_yield_distributed` | Protocol-wide cumulative yield. |
| Vault balance | `connection.getBalance(vaultPda)` — your numerator. |
