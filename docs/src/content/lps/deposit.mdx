# Deposit SOL

<p className="lede">
  Deposit SOL into the vault. You get shares back. Shares grow in value as borrowers repay. There is no minimum and no lockup.
</p>

<Callout kind="tldr">
  **tl;dr —** `deposit(amount)` → SOL moves to the `vault` PDA → shares are minted to your `DepositorRecord` PDA → you accrue yield as borrowers repay interest.
</Callout>

## How shares work

Shares represent a fractional claim on the vault. The vault grows when borrowers repay interest (and when defaulted loans contribute their admin fee and reclaimed rent). Your share count stays constant; the SOL you can redeem per share goes up.

Mechanically it's the same as a Compound cToken or ERC-4626 vault. See [Shares & yield](/lps/shares) for the math.

## Doing it

The web app at [app.solignition.xyz](https://app.solignition.xyz) is the supported path for LPs today. Connect your wallet, choose an amount, sign the deposit transaction. The app handles the PDA derivations.

For programmatic deposits (scripts, bots, custom UIs), call the `deposit` instruction directly:

```ts
await program.methods
  .deposit(new BN(amount_lamports))
  .accounts({ depositor: wallet.publicKey })
  .rpc()
```

Anchor derives the rest (`depositor_record`, `protocol_config`, `vault`, `system_program`) from PDA seeds. See [On-chain program](/reference/program) for the full account list.

## What's stored

After your first deposit, you have a `DepositorRecord` account at PDA seeds `["depositor", your_pubkey]`. It holds:

- `owner` — your pubkey (must sign withdrawals).
- `deposited_amount` — cumulative lamports you've deposited.
- `share_amount` — your current share balance.
- `last_update_ts` — last time the record was touched.

Subsequent deposits update the same record (your shares add up).

## Minimums and limits

- **No minimum.** Any positive `amount` works.
- **No maximum.** Bounded only by your wallet balance.
- **No lockup.** You can withdraw immediately, subject to vault liquidity.
- **No fee.** Deposits don't pay anything to the protocol.

## What can go wrong

| Error | Cause |
|---|---|
| `ProtocolPaused` (6000) | Admin paused the protocol. |
| `InvalidAmount` (6001) | You passed `0` or an amount that overflows in share math. |
| `InsufficientBalance` (6002) | Your wallet doesn't have the SOL. |

## After you deposit

Watch the `Deposited` event for confirmation:

```
{
  depositor: <your pubkey>,
  amount: <lamports you deposited>,
  total_shares: <your new share balance>
}
```
